Privacy Policy

GymRank (iOS) / Last updated October 6, 2026

What we do not collect

GymRank does not collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number. There is no account registration. The app contains no usage analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

The sex, body weight, goal, training frequency, and experience you enter in the first questions, and the workouts you log (exercise, weight, reps, date and time), are stored only on your device and are not sent anywhere. Rank and score calculations are all done on your device.

If you delete the app, all records stored on the device are deleted. If you have device backups turned on (such as iCloud Backup), iOS includes this data in those backups.

What other users can see in the Weekly League

If you join the Weekly League, only the following information is stored in Apple's iCloud (the CloudKit public database) and shown to other users in the same group:

  • An auto-generated display name (for example, "Gold Titan#4821"). Your real name and account name are never used.
  • Your XP for the week (points calculated from how much you logged) and your position
  • Your tier (for example, "Gold I")

To assign groups and order the standings, the app also stores which week and group you are in (for example, "2026 week 40, Gold tier, group 1") and a random number that the app creates for each device. These are not shown to other users and contain no information about who you are. The random number is recreated when you delete your league data.

Your body weight, height, sex, and individual records (weights and reps) are never sent to the league. The Weekly League works only when you are signed in to iCloud. League records are linked to an anonymous identifier that Apple issues for this app only; the developer never learns your Apple Account name or email address.

You can delete your league data from the Me tab in the app.

Network communication

The only network communication this app performs is with iCloud (CloudKit) for the Weekly League, and with the App Store to confirm purchases and restore purchases. Both go through Apple's systems, and Apple's handling of that information is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Share cards

When you rank up, you decide where to send the share card (an image). The app never sends it automatically.

Sharing with third parties

Because we do not collect information that identifies you, there is nothing to provide or sell to third parties.

Children

The app does not ask for your age.

Information for users in the European Economic Area (GDPR)

Controller. The controller of the Weekly League data described above is the developer of GymRank: Takayuki Zukeran, an individual developer based in Japan. Contact details are under "Contact" below.

Purpose and legal basis. Weekly League data (display name, weekly XP, tier, week and group, the random device number, and the CloudKit anonymous identifier) is processed only to run the Weekly League you choose to join: placing you in a group, ranking members, and showing the standings. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: the processing is necessary to provide the feature you ask for. Data that stays on your device is not processed by the developer.

Recipients and storage location. Weekly League data is stored by Apple in iCloud (CloudKit public database) and shown to other members of your group. Because the developer is based in Japan and iCloud is operated by Apple, this data may be processed outside the EEA. Japan is covered by an EU adequacy decision, and Apple processes iCloud data under its own data protection terms.

Retention. Weekly League data remains until you delete it from the Me tab. It is not deleted automatically. If you stop using the league without deleting it, your past weekly entries stay in the league records, and you can still delete them from the Me tab at any time.

Your rights. Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can delete your Weekly League data yourself at any time from the Me tab; deleting it removes you from the league. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country of residence.

No automated decisions. Your tier and score are calculated on your device for display only and have no legal or similarly significant effect on you.
