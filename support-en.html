FAQ

How is my rank calculated?

From the weight and reps you log, the app estimates your one-rep max (1RM), compares it to your body weight against a standard for each exercise, and turns it into a score from 1 to 1000. Your overall rank combines the scores of each muscle group. Logging more sets never lowers your rank.

What can I use for free?

Logging, your overall rank, the muscle map, the rank-up screen, share cards, and the Weekly League are free. With Pro (monthly or yearly) you also get scores and progress charts for each exercise, and how much more weight you need for your next rank.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open the Settings app → your name (Apple Account) → Subscriptions. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, you won't be charged for the next one.

I got a new iPhone. Do I need to buy Pro again?

No. With the same Apple Account, tap "Restore Purchases" on the Me tab. Your workout history is stored on your device, so move it to the new iPhone with an iPhone backup.

I can't join the Weekly League

The Weekly League requires being signed in to iCloud. Sign in to iCloud in the Settings app, then open the League tab again.

Is my body weight or workout history sent anywhere?

No. Only your automatic display name, weekly XP, and tier appear in the Weekly League. See the Privacy Policy for details.
